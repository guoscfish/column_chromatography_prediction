# Frozen protocol

Primary evidence is compound-grouped nested OOF on the inherited filtered gradient-train identities; row OOF is secondary. M3 is compared independently with `CENTER_MAGNITUDE` and `CENTER_CONDITIONED_WIDTH`. The gate was frozen before producing candidate results. Even a passing candidate is only marked eligible: this runner never reads outer truth.

The gate requires at least 3% aggregate normalized-RMSE gain, positive RMSE gain in at least three of four column-endpoints, at least 3% gain in two endpoints, no endpoint mean deterioration over 5%, at least 3/5 wins for each positively improved endpoint, and no seed-context normalized-RMSE deterioration over 10%.
