# Filtered transfer headroom audit

A preregistered, train-only audit of M0 scale, M1 Conditional EA, M2 source-magnitude varying slope, and M3 center/width transfer on the filtered 25g/40g population. Outer test truth is not read unless a candidate first passes the protocol gate.

## Superseding methodological note

The [controlled lightweight transfer audit](../controlled_lightweight_transfer_audit/FINAL_REPORT.md) supersedes model-comparison claims from this preliminary study while preserving all frozen numerical artifacts here. The old M2 was not a strict nested extension of Conditional EA because its loss normalization and Ridge priors differed. Its negative aggregate gain means it **worsened**, rather than decreased, normalized RMSE by about 3.8%. The true M3 coefficient count is 6 (three center plus three width), not 8.
