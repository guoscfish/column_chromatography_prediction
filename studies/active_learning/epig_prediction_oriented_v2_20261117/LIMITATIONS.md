# Scope and limits

This is post-stage1/stage2 exploratory development on a repeatedly exposed cohort. Old qualification failures remain failures under their unchanged thresholds. The primary GP is intentionally fixed before new learning outcomes to isolate the objective; the finite feature/model diagnostics cannot retroactively select a favorable main surrogate.

All five matched seed pairs reached L1005 in 210 successful downstream fits. `RESULTS.md` and the completion receipts record the full-window validation comparison. The runs used 11.55 hours of active QGeoGNN fitting time and 0.59 hours of online auxiliary computation. These times describe this machine and include the one user interruption recorded in the ledger.

The main probabilistic model is empirical-Bayes independent-output isotropic Matérn with constant mean and homoscedastic Gaussian noise. Hyperparameter, mean and noise uncertainty are not integrated. New F0/F1/F2 diagnostics are bounded and show no universal probability-quality fix. ARD, nonconstant GP means, heteroscedastic noise and robust likelihoods are not claimed tested merely because reviewed. Non-Gaussian models need a newly derived/verified MI estimator. Global covariance congruence cannot improve same-posterior EPIG ranks.

OOF evaluates models trained on222 rows, not the exact posterior fitted to all333 or later1005 rows. Row splits share molecules and outer seeds overlap. Probability intervals and Wilson summaries are descriptive under row/fold dependence. Cross-sample conditional logscore conditions on other known held-out L333 labels as a diagnostic; it is not an acquisition-time oracle or honest independent future test experiment. Full/block/diagonal NLPD decomposition distinguishes within-row endpoint dependence from cross-row information but does not uniquely identify a true causal joint distribution.

Encoder-BLR feasibility is conditional on a supervised L333 encoder. It is not honest pipeline OOF and cannot pass the original gate. No fresh cross-fitted GNN encoders or changed main predictor were used. Quantiles were never treated as cross-input covariance.

Exact reference integration concerns the finite declared population. P1 includes observed-X future replicates; P2 is current-U X. These are distinct tasks and neither is asserted to be the unknown deployment distribution. Coupled subsampling reduces an identified confound; it does not make P1/P2 draws identical. Offline reference evidence covers the five L333 states, not every future trajectory state. The disk-backed independent-channel implementation is specialized to independent outputs; generic correlated-output correctness is checked on small Gaussian oracles. Mapped pages can reside in RAM; reported RSS, not an ideal O(chunk) claim, governs resource evidence. Extreme rotated near-singular q2 matrices expose float64 cancellation in the original fast determinant branch; the actual independent-output primary path uses channelwise log1p.

IVR uses fixed-L333-standardized endpoint variance and EPIG is invariant to invertible output units. An objective comparison therefore includes that deliberate loss weighting. Batch MI is average per-target information about the chosen batch, not stacked-target joint MI or true reduction in QGeoGNN risk. Greedy B32 has no global-optimality guarantee. No full-training BALD or pointwise-B32 arm substitutes for the necessary EPIG/IVR budget.

Historical Random/LCMD/Kernel-IVR/Hybrid/Free LLM results remain read-only context. Their row boundaries, budgets, model protocol and exception histories must be checked before strict ranking. No pooled superiority is claimed by this study without that check. Validation is reused for checkpoint selection and curve evaluation; it is not a fresh independent test. Test labels were not evaluated, paid LLM APIs were not called and Free LLM efficacy was not newly tested.

Only210 primary successes are authorized by the current runner, with231 attempt maximum, finite per-key retries,72 training/12 auxiliary active hours and conservative offline reservations. A paired L1485 extension needs a new sealed protocol/resource budget; absent that extension, no statement about long-run EPIG validity follows from an L1005 result. A favorable primary result alone does not establish reliable incremental assistance to Free LLM.
