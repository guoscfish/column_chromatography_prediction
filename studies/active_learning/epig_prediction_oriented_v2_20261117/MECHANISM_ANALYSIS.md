# Mechanisms and actual learning evidence

This exploratory study separates mathematically valid acquisition, proxy quality, finite-reference policy stability, and actual QGeoGNN learning. `RESULTS.md` is generated from verified successful fits and remains the authority for real Row-level efficacy; missing trajectories are not negative findings.

## M1: objective at a common posterior

All five full-P1 L333 states use the same original Matérn posterior within each seed for EPIG/IVR/BALD. EPIG–IVR singleton Spearman is0.9642–0.9939 but conditional B32 overlap is21–25/32. EPIG batches have greater integrated target MI in all five measured comparisons, by construction the criterion it optimizes; the differences are small in absolute nats. This proves the policies remain distinct after exact reference integration, not that their QGeoGNN learning outcomes differ. The new real trajectories use the same modeling rules, with different fitted posteriors after L diverges.

## M2: batch redundancy

The independent dense-B Schur calculation agrees with summed conditional gains for70 reference states (max error5.55e−17 nats). Full-P1 greedy EPIG B32 yields more true proxy batch MI than pointwise Top32 for all five measured seeds. Pointwise sums overstate independent information when candidate labels are correlated. This supports conditionalization for this proxy. No separate full five-seed pointwise training arm was run; a causal effect on Row AULC is still unmeasured.

## M3: target population and sampling

Full P1/P2 EPIG B32 overlap averages0.96875. The old independently salted M1024 P1/P2 overlap is0.1375; common ordering raises it to0.725. Thus a large part of the old apparent target-distribution effect was reference sampling. M1024 and even M2048 can substantially change greedy sets relative to the exact population. High singleton Spearman does not certify B32 convergence. Primary v2 uses exact P1; this result concerns L333 states only and does not prove future states identical.

## M4: surrogate versus predictor

Sixty new honest OOF fits test a finite feature/optimization family; five additional frozen-encoder BLR fits are explicitly conditional diagnostics. Input features and even single-point EPIG Top32 change, but NLL does not uniformly improve. Extra optimization nearly reproduces old F0 outcomes, so a universal optimization-convergence failure is not supported. Inner nested GP noise-bound sensitivity and sharp-center/heavy-tail residuals remain specific concerns. The encoder direction supplies a legal joint covariance, but an encoder trained on all L333 cannot provide honest L333 OOF probability qualification.

No future-U oracle utility was computed. Main-run label revelations occur only after immutable legal selections. Only completed scratch-retraining branches measure true learning returns. The available comparison cannot causally distinguish objective mismatch, conditional-noise misspecification, limited input support and GNN optimization/representation as a unique failure source. A stronger one-step mechanism study requires separately frozen common-state counterfactual branches and finite authorized label budgets.

## M5: condition learning

Original F0 and QGeoGNN both encode sample volume through density×volume. Separate density/volume are absent, but observed F0 contains no exact collisions with different omitted density/volume; the demonstrated issue is representation bias/extrapolation, not measured information loss. Input-only repeats, PE/EA, loading-solvent volume and sample-volume directions are documented in `FEATURE_AND_SURROGATE_AUDIT.md` and the old hash-verified `PREDICTOR_MATCH_DIAGNOSTICS.md`. Small frozen predicted responses do not establish small physical effects or a failure to learn after acquisition. Historical label-exposed audits cannot be combined with current unexposed U to manufacture oracle gains.

The original predictor is held fixed for the main comparison. Any future condition-branch intervention must be applied symmetrically to EPIG and the control and treated as another experimental factor. No main-predictor change was made here.

## What the full trajectories can resolve

All five paired trajectories reached L1005. EPIG lowered the full-window validation AULC in every seed. The mean difference was −0.01527, or 2.54% of the IVR mean. The endpoint showed more seed variation. EPIG lowered L1005 NRMSE in seeds 157 and 12203, while IVR had the lower NRMSE in seeds 887, 2357, and 6101. These results describe the two acquisition procedures under the fixed proxy and refit protocol on five overlapping splits. The study design does not attribute the differences to proxy calibration, batch redundancy, or individual condition directions. `RESULTS.md` and `paired_differences.csv` report the observed effects.
